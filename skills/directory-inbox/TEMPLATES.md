# Reply skeletons

Fill `<…>` from the letter and the checks. Rewrite in the sender's language and
in the directory's voice; run `stop-slop` before showing the draft. The badge
paragraph is the same in every template on purpose: optional, one way among
others, never a condition.

## Badge paragraph (shared)

> If you would like a "Verified on <Directory>" badge on your site, the snippet
> below links to your profile. It is one way to confirm the listing is yours;
> a reply from an address on <domain> works just as well. The listing stays
> either way.
>
> `<a href="https://<directory>/<section>/<slug>/">Verified on <Directory></a>`

## Claim: profile exists

> Hi <name>,
>
> Thanks for getting in touch. Your profile is here:
> https://<directory>/<section>/<slug>/, listed under
> <category> (https://<directory>/<category-path>/).
>
> <If From is on their domain:> Your address is on <domain>, so we have marked
> the profile as verified. <Else:> To mark it verified, reply from an address on
> <domain>, or use the badge below.
>
> Send any corrections (services, location, contact) and we will update it.
>
> <Badge paragraph>

## Add us: accepted

> Hi <name>,
>
> Thanks, <business> is now listed:
> https://<directory>/<section>/<slug>/, in <category>
> (https://<directory>/<category-path>/).
>
> <Verified sentence as in Claim.>
>
> <Badge paragraph>

(Send this only once the directory session has published the profile; until
then: "we are adding it, you will get the link in a day or two".)

## Add us / claim: declined

> Hi <name>,
>
> Thanks for writing. <Directory> lists <what the directory covers>, and
> <business> is outside that for now <one concrete reason: no public site,
> different field>. If that changes, write again and we will take another look.

## Live reply to our outreach

Answer what they said, in their terms, in three to five sentences; no template
paragraph. Offer the one next step the outreach was for. Log them as a warm
contact.
