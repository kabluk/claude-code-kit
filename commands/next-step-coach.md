# Next Step Coach

Context: $ARGUMENTS

Give exactly 2 or 3 next options:
1. the best logical step;
2. a safe alternative;
3. a check, documentation or preparation step, only when it is useful.

Then:

```text
Term of the day: TERM — expansion, if it is an acronym.
In plain words: one sentence.
Example: one practical sentence.
Remember: a short mnemonic.
```

Pick a term related to the work just done: CLI, API, REST, HTTP, DNS, CDN, CORS, CRUD, JWT, OAuth, SDK, DOM, SSR, CSR, SPA, ORM, SQL, CI/CD, PR, Git, merge, rebase, rollback, lint, typecheck, E2E, cache, queue, webhook, middleware, endpoint, payload, schema, migration, container, Docker, IaC, observability, tracing, DAG, workflow, loop, idempotency, latency, retry, fallback, monorepo, microservice, serverless, reverse proxy, WebSocket, TTL, context window, agent, subagent, hook, dogfooding, fan-out.

Check the latest entries in `docs/project/LEARNING_LOG.md`, do not repeat a recent term, and append a new line:
`YYYY-MM-DD | TERM | short meaning`.
